About Hashim Jadoon

A dedicated advocate born and raised in the serene village of Ghumawan, Hashim Jadoon returned home after graduating from the prestigious University of London in 2022. With a High Court licence and experience in Islamabad's legal circuit, Hashim has emerged as a fierce defender of environmental rights and community justice.

At just under 30, he has already transformed his village by challenging powerful interests, proving that one lawyer's resolve can crush injustice.

"I represent one village, one municipality. What of the others? The law should be the deterrent, not me."

Hashim Jadoon

Areas of Expertise

Environmental Law

Specializing in cases involving illegal mining, land degradation, and protection under the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Environmental Protection Act.

Community Rights

Advocating for local communities against unlawful taxes, unapproved developments, and corporate overreach.

Public Interest Litigation

Representing marginalized groups in high-stakes cases before the Peshawar High Court and Supreme Court.

Landmark Victories

Ghumawan Crush Plants Sealed

Filed case in June 2022 leading to sealing in March 2023. Peshawar High Court dismissed petitions on March 11, 2025, restraining operations for lacking environmental approvals.

Supreme Court Ruling on Illegal Mining

Represented Haripur case reaching SC; July 11, 2024 ruling directed Environmental Protection Orders against over 900 illegal crush plants in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Landmark Judgment Preserving Mountains

May 19, 2025 judgment declared crush plants illegal, calling for mountain preservation and sparking a movement for environmental justice.
