About Hashim Jadoon

A voice for the voiceless, a defender of our environment

Advocate Supreme Court

Born and raised in the serene village of Ghumawan in Haripur District, Hashim Jadoon represents a new generation of Pakistani lawyers who understand that true justice extends beyond courtrooms—it encompasses our duty to protect the environment for future generations.

World-Class Education
LLB (Hons.) in Law - University of London (2022)
Bachelor of Commerce - University of Toronto
Licensed Advocate - High Court of Islamabad & Peshawar

After earning his law degree from the prestigious University of London in 2022 and obtaining his High Court licence, Hashim returned home with a mission. While many lawyers seek the prestige of corporate firms or lucrative commercial practice, Hashim chose to defend his community against powerful mining interests that were systematically destroying the mountains he grew up beneath.

Starting his career at a top-tier law firm handling complex corporate and constitutional cases, Hashim gained invaluable experience appearing before District Courts, High Courts (Islamabad & Peshawar), and the Supreme Court of Pakistan in civil, banking, commercial, and environmental matters. This solid foundation in both contentious and non-contentious legal work equipped him with the expertise to take on seemingly impossible challenges.

At just under 30 years old, he has achieved what many consider impossible: single-handedly taking on over 900 illegal crush plants across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, securing landmark judgments from the Supreme Court of Pakistan, and sparking a nationwide movement for environmental justice. He is the first lawyer to successfully challenge and win against the Cantonment Board's property tax authority in Abbottabad—a historic victory for property owners' rights.

Landmark Victories

Precedent-setting cases that changed environmental law in Pakistan

Historic Mountain Preservation Judgment (May 19, 2025)

Secured the most comprehensive environmental protection judgment in Pakistani history. The court declared crush plants operating without proper environmental clearances illegal and called for urgent mountain preservation measures. This ruling has sparked a nationwide movement for environmental justice and established new standards for protecting Pakistan's natural heritage.

Impact: Nationwide Precedent for Environmental Protection

Legal Significance: This judgment has become a blueprint for environmental advocates across Pakistan, demonstrating that dedicated legal action can overcome even the most entrenched industrial interests when the law is properly applied.

Ghumawan Crush Plants Sealed (2023-2025)

Filed groundbreaking case in June 2022 that led to the sealing of multiple illegal crush plants in Ghumawan. Despite fierce opposition from powerful mining interests, the Peshawar High Court dismissed all petitions on March 11, 2025, definitively restraining operations due to lack of environmental approvals.

Supreme Court Ruling on Illegal Mining (2024)

Successfully represented the Haripur case before Pakistan's Supreme Court. The landmark judgment of July 11, 2024 directed Environmental Protection Orders against over 900 illegal crush plants across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, setting a nationwide precedent.
