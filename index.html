About Hashim Jadoon

A voice for the voiceless, a defender of our environment

Born and raised in the serene village of Ghumawan in Haripur District, Hashim Jadoon represents a new generation of Pakistani lawyers who understand that true justice extends beyond courtrooms—it encompasses our duty to protect the environment for future generations.

After earning his law degree from the prestigious University of London in 2022 and obtaining his High Court licence, Hashim returned home with a mission. While many lawyers seek the prestige of corporate firms, Hashim chose to defend his community against powerful mining interests that were systematically destroying the mountains he grew up beneath.

At just under 30 years old, he has achieved what many consider impossible: single-handedly taking on over 900 illegal crush plants across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, securing landmark judgments from the Supreme Court of Pakistan, and sparking a nationwide movement for environmental justice.

"I represent one village, one municipality. What of the others? The law should be the deterrent, not me."

— Hashim Jadoon

Landmark Victories

Precedent-setting cases that changed environmental law in Pakistan

Ghumawan Crush Plants Sealed (2023-2025)

Filed groundbreaking case in June 2022 that led to the sealing of multiple illegal crush plants in Ghumawan. Despite fierce opposition from powerful mining interests, the Peshawar High Court dismissed all petitions on March 11, 2025, definitively restraining operations due to lack of environmental approvals.

"Hashim bhai was truly a one man army, and for that I am eternally grateful." — Adnan, Local Resident

Supreme Court Ruling on Illegal Mining (2024)

Successfully represented the Haripur case before Pakistan's Supreme Court. The landmark judgment of July 11, 2024 directed Environmental Protection Orders against over 900 illegal crush plants across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, setting a nationwide precedent.

Historic Mountain Preservation Judgment (May 19, 2025)

Secured the most comprehensive environmental protection judgment in Pakistani history. The court declared crush plants operating without proper environmental clearances illegal and called for urgent mountain preservation measures. This ruling has sparked a nationwide movement for environmental justice and established new standards for protecting Pakistan's natural heritage.

Impact: This judgment has become a blueprint for environmental advocates across Pakistan, demonstrating that dedicated legal action can overcome even the most entrenched industrial interests when the law is properly applied.
